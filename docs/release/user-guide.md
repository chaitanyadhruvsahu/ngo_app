# NGO pilot user guide

Pilot link: **to be filled after deployment**. Support contact: **to be assigned**.

Use the link in your phone or computer browser. No APK installation is needed for this version. Internet access is required. For the initial trial, use only sample names, photos and documents: never real Aadhaar, bank details or beneficiary records.

## Fellows

1. Choose English or Hindi, then create an account with an email address you can access.
2. Open the verification email and follow the link. Check spam if it does not arrive; ask support if delivery fails. Do not forward your verification link.
3. Sign in and complete your profile. Use the visible **Save** action and wait for confirmation before leaving.
4. Open **Documents**, choose the required document type and a supported file, review the selected filename, then upload. Wait for the uploaded record to appear. Files above 10 MB must be reduced first; renaming a file does not convert its format.
5. The home page shows the next incomplete step. Once onboarding and profile completion requirements are met, it shows one **All set** badge. This is not a legal identity certification or a claim that the NGO manually approved every record.
6. Open **Jobs**, select an assigned job and follow its instructions. Use the photo action, allow camera permission when needed, review the photo and wait for submission confirmation. If permission is denied, enable camera access in browser settings or use an available file-selection option.
7. Open **Volunteers** to add or update your volunteer records. Use only records you are authorized to manage.
8. Open **Notifications** to read announcements/reminders. Sign out on shared devices.

Forgot your password? Use the password-reset option on the sign-in page. Open the newest reset email, choose a password of at least 10 characters, then sign in again. Links expire and cannot be reused after a successful reset.

## NGO staff

An administrator account is created by the deployment owner; do not register a fellow account expecting staff access.

- **Fellows:** search/filter and open a fellow to review their profile and permitted documents.
- **Jobs:** create a job, assign recipients, set its due date and review submitted work/photos. Test with one sample fellow first.
- **Notifications:** send or schedule an announcement for the intended audience. Check recipients and time before confirming.
- **Reports:** use the dashboard and available exports. Downloaded reports may contain personal data: store them securely and do not circulate them in open messaging groups.

## A first 20-minute demonstration

Use two separate browser sessions: one admin and one sample fellow. Create and verify the fellow; save their sample profile; upload a harmless sample document; assign one job; submit a sample photo; send an announcement; check the report. Finally sign out and sign back in, confirming that the records remain. Try the same workflow on a phone, including the keyboard and camera.

## If something goes wrong

- Lost connection: reconnect and check whether the item was saved before trying again. Avoid repeated taps on Submit/Upload.
- Upload failed: check file type, size and internet connection. Keep your original file until upload is confirmed.
- Page looks stale after an announced update: reload it; if necessary close and reopen the tab. Report repeated problems.
- Access denied: contact your NGO coordinator; do not share another person's login.
- Send support the screen name, time, device/browser, action taken, expected result and exact error text. Redact screenshots. Never send passwords, verification links or identity/bank documents in a bug report.
